About Quiet Skies

A map of recorded flights over the Northeast, with a modelled estimate of how loud each one was on the ground.

What the map shows

  • Every line is one recorded flight, coloured by height above the ground. Where lines pile up, traffic is heavy.
  • Hatched areas are unknown, not quiet. There, no aircraft was recorded below 1,500 ft above the ground within about 10 km. Either none flew that low, or none could be heard because no receiver was in range. The data cannot tell the two apart.
  • The noise footprint is a model. It is built from each aircraft type's certified noise at 394 ft, with spreading and air absorption. It leaves out terrain, weather and climb-out adjustments. It is an estimate, not a measurement.
  • People counts on a flight's card are residents of the 2 km squares where that flight reached a given sound level, from census counts. They are not people who were outside, and they depend on modelled levels. Treat them as rough.

The data and its limits

  • 136,450 recorded flights over 7 days (Oct 29 to Nov 4, 2026); the first and last days are partial.
  • Small planes without ADS-B, and some military flights, are never recorded. Aircraft below the range of the receivers are missing too, which is what the hatching shows.
  • Altitudes are barometric. Ground height comes from a terrain model of about 220 m resolution.
  • Coverage: the region is split into one data file set per state or province, loaded when you tick it. One week is a snapshot, not a long-term picture.

Aircraft registered to companies, agencies and governments show their registration, owner and address on a flight's card. Anything else, including every aircraft registered to a private person, shows only its type, model and year; the registration, address and owner are withheld.

Using the map

Scroll or pinch to zoom and drag to move. The panel on the right (Stats & filters) has the spot numbers for the point under the pointer, and filters for states, aircraft, altitude, airports, days and hours. The Save image button saves the visible map as a picture. Your settings are kept in your browser only.
