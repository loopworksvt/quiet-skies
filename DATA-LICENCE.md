# Data licence

The flight data in `packs/` is derived from [adsb.lol](https://adsb.lol) open ADS-B data and is made available under the
[Open Database License (ODbL) v1.0](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/). You are free to copy, share and adapt it, as long as you credit adsb.lol,
keep the same licence for anything you build from it, and keep it open.

Other sources and their licences are listed on the About page (`about.html`).

The sound levels are modelled estimates, not measurements.
